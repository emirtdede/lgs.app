# 01 — Product Vision

## Problem

The student needs to build academic foundations from near-zero, follow a long-term LGS preparation program consistently, see objective self-improvement, and let family members monitor progress without turning the process into pressure or surveillance.

## Outcome

The product answers four questions reliably:

1. **Student:** “Bugün ne yapacağım?”
2. **Student:** “Geçmiş halime göre nasıl gelişiyorum?”
3. **Family:** “Plan uygulanıyor mu; nerede destek gerekiyor?”
4. **Admin:** “Planı bozmadan kaçırılan işi nasıl yeniden planlarım?”

## Success principles

- Consistency over intensity.
- Understanding before speed.
- Speed is observed, not forced.
- Accuracy and speed are paired.
- Foundations before grade-level shortcuts.
- Plan integrity is more important than gamification.
- Private-by-default.
- Student UI stays calm and simple.

## Non-goals

- school homework manager
- school exam planner
- public learning network
- AI tutor/chatbot
- parent-control surveillance tool
- commercial SaaS billing
- class ranking
- teacher LMS
