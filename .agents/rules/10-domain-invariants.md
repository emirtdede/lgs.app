# LGS Domain Invariants

The plan is school-independent. Weekday availability begins 16:00; sleep is 22:00; no new required session after 21:50. Math/Turkish user-selected playlists are fixed. Daily Math and Paragraph begin at >=20 questions. Speed comparison always uses an exact 20-question Benchmark block; target-above-20 extras are separate. Timer counts up only; no target time/countdown. Record correct/wrong/blank with duration. 2026-10-01 Math is a non-mastery baseline. Topic questions are separate from daily routines. Video alone never completes a topic. MEB questions only for learned topics. Missed tasks do not auto-roll to tomorrow; admin explicitly reschedules with audit. Reading is optional, unlocked after all required effective tasks are completed or explicitly cancelled/excused, and excluded from completion percentage. Cancellation is never mastery; an all-cancelled day is excused from consistency. Verified calendar-only availability overrides may change effective time placement but never academic order/mastery. No peer ranking, public profiles, shame language, ads or gamification pressure.
